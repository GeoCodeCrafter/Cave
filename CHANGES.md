# changes

- first version: chambers + spaghetti tunnels with terrain, generates around players, ore on walls
- can generate in edit mode now with Cave.preview() so you can actually look at it, Cave.clear() wipes it
- rock layers by depth (sandstone > limestone > slate > basalt) with wavy edges, rock patches, dirt floors, lava at the bottom, custom colours
- ores were buried inside the walls, now they're clumps of chunks stuck on the wall surface poking into the tunnel. gold is foil, diamond is glass
- kept spawning inside solid rock so you never actually saw a tunnel. findSpawn() looks for air with a floor, preview() puts the camera there and play mode spawns you there
- tried raycasting ores onto the walls, raycasts miss fresh terrain in edit mode so 0 ores. added Cave.check() which showed the terrain matches the code 300/300, so ores go straight on the voxel face again
- new cave shape that's always connected: one node per chunk, tunnels to the next node east and south, some ramps down a level, some chambers, all bent with noise. noise blobs could never guarantee that
- first go at the network still left 84 sealed pockets: tunnels got thinner than a voxel and the bending tore them. gentler bend + 4 stud minimum radius = one connected cave, 0 pockets
- caves were too narrow and too grid-like. wider tunnels (8), bigger chambers, nodes spread across more of the chunk, south links only 60% of the time plus some diagonals. still one connected cave in the sim, 0 sealed pockets
- added Cave.measure() to find out where roblox actually draws the wall, run it in play mode
- ores were still a couple of studs inside the rock, roblox draws the wall further into the tunnel than the maths. added oreOffset to push them out
- lighting: a lantern on a chain at every tunnel junction, gold and diamond glow, dark warm ambient, haze, bloom, slight colour grade
- mining: click ore to hit it (coal 3 hits up to diamond 12), it shakes and throws chips, breaks into bits on the last hit, goes on the leaderboard, grows back later. server checks reach and a cooldown
- kept falling forever on play: the server teleported you before the terrain reached your computer. added a spawn room (plank floor, beams, lamp, real SpawnLocation) dug out at a junction, and players only spawn once the mine is built and streamed in. respawns you there when you die
- spawn room was cramped, terrain smoothing crept over the floor. now 40x40 with a dome, dug out under the floor too
- walls: thin stripes running through each rock layer, less saturated colours, lanterns and fog more neutral so it's not all one orange
- big caverns: 1 junction in 14 gets a huge flattened dome (75-110 studs across) with 2-4 rock pillars instead of a chamber. pillars left a few 1-voxel sealed pockets in the sim so lone air voxels get filled back in
- biomes: big regions of crystal caves (basalt + violet ice walls, glowing neon crystals, pale floor) and mushroom caves (mud walls, mossy floor, glowing mushrooms). both light themselves so no lanterns there. Cave.findBiome("crystal") jumps to one
- digging: hold click on rock to carve through it, dust in the rock's colour. server checks reach, cooldown and the top of the mine. (first version also blocked the spawn room walls, which is exactly where you test it, so that's gone) Dig.client.luau goes in StarterPlayerScripts
- decoration in plain rock: stalactites (walk-through), stalagmites, rubble and small still water puddles. spikes and rubble take the colour of the rock around them
- performance: nearest chunks load first, chunks far from everyone get cleared (spawn room never), digging is remembered and put back when a chunk reloads, lights further than 110 studs from every player switch off
- digging next to ore used to leave it floating. now dug-out ore breaks and counts as mined (no regrow, its rock is gone), crystals/spikes/rubble/lanterns shatter, and reloaded chunks don't put anything back inside a dug hole
- ore counts save with DataStore: on leaving, every 60s, and on shutdown. a failed load never saves over your real counts, and anything mined while loading gets added on top
- readme: took the collage out, gold screenshot back on top
- ore used to grow back straight into whoever was standing there. now it waits until the spot's clear, checking every 2 seconds
- pickaxe: built from parts, everyone spawns with one. hold click to swing about 3 times a second, hitting ore mines it and hitting rock digs. swing is the grip tilting, no animation asset. optional hit sound in settings.hitSound
- readme feature list caught up with everything since lighting, and the api setting is under Experience Settings now
- rojo: default.project.json puts the three scripts where they go and sets lighting to Future. rokit.toml pins rojo 7.6.1 to match the plugin
- flooded biome: comes out of the plain rock on its own noise map. each layer has a flat water line and the tunnels under it fill with real water, but only columns with rock underneath (water doesn't flow, it'd float). cobblestone walls, sand floors, glowing algae instead of lanterns. (first go used Pebble for the walls, which is a part material not a terrain one, so it crashed before building anything)
- tide pool biome instead: flooded tunnels were just big flat water sheets and bright green walls. now it's dry grey caves with small pools sunk into the floor, clear water, wet stones round the rim and glowing anemones at the bottom. water's clearer everywhere so you can see in
- chasms: long ravines cut down through several layers, wide at the top and narrowing, walls bent like the tunnels. sagging rope bridges wherever a tunnel meets one, with the ends found by walking out until there's floor
- starting area: spawn room gets rails and a minecart full of coal pointing at the first tunnel, crates, barrels, a tool rack and a sign with the controls. the first tunnel always has lanterns down it and a couple of coal and iron so you find something straight away
- new mine every server: random seed, shown in the corner. LOCK_SEED in Start.server.luau keeps it the same
- first tunnel's ore and lanterns moved further out, the dug-out spawn room reached them and left the ore floating
- tidied the repo: scripts in src/ (Test.server.luau is Start.server.luau now, Dig.client.luau is Pickaxe.client.luau), MIT licence, readme rewritten
- it's public on roblox now, play link at the top of the readme
- glowworms: cavern ceilings get clusters of tiny blue lights and a few hanging threads with a bead on the end
- properly dark now away from lights. everyone gets a helmet lamp, and F throws a flare that bounces, sparks and burns for 20 seconds (5 each max, short cooldown)
- cave walls: rough rock instead of smooth tubes (two layers of bumps), faint shelves along the walls in places, flatter floors with arched ceilings, and half-buried boulders. small sealed air bubbles get filled back in per chunk. (the caverns entry above said lone voxels already got filled, that was only ever in the sim, this is the first time the game actually does it)
- atmosphere per biome: the fog, colour grade and floating particles fade to match where you're standing. violet sparkles in crystal caves, green spores with the mushrooms, cool droplets by the tide pools, embers down in the lava. new Atmosphere.client.luau does it, the server just tells it the biome
- custom rock texture: Stylized Rock PBR Material by Nezhull (public domain) on Rock, Slate, Sandstone and Limestone (just rock and slate at first, but the top layers where you spend most time are sandstone and limestone so you couldn't see it), through MaterialService overrides so terrain picks it up. first insert attempts failed with "Authentication required to access Asset", studio's login had gone stale, logging out and back in fixed it
- props are real meshes now, built from code with EditableMesh: wobbly cones for stalactites and stalagmites, six-sided shards for crystals, lumpy rocks for ore and rubble. 18 shapes made once at start and cloned with random size and turn, since roblox caps code-built mesh. falls back to the old blocks if meshes aren't allowed
- underground rivers: each one runs from one junction to another a few chunks away, winding side to side and always downhill. real terrain water in the bottom of the channel, only where there's rock under it. foam drifts downstream, and where a river runs out over a chasm or cavern there's a waterfall. Cave.findRiver() takes you to one
- about 1 ore clump in 10 was floating. the 2 stud push off the wall was set for the old smooth tunnels, on rough walls it shoved ore off the bumps. tried 2, 1 and 0.5 over the same 703 clumps: 68, 14 and 2 floating, none buried, so it's 0.5 now
- the code-built meshes only ever worked in Studio. in a real game players got red and green checker boxes, because meshes made on the server don't reach them (i'd only checked the part arrived, not its shape). now the shapes live in ReplicatedStorage.CaveMeshes, the server places plain stand-in blocks tagged with a shape, and each player's new Props.client.luau builds the meshes itself and swaps them in
- rivers crossing an open cave just looked like a flat blue puddle, nothing to hold the water in. now they get a low bank of the local rock either side there, and sand on the riverbed
- rivers still read as a flat navy slab: reflective water in a dark cave just mirrors the dark. water's lighter, clearer and barely reflective now, with small moving waves, soft lights under the surface along the river, brighter foam, and rivers narrowed from 14 to 10 studs
- sinkholes: round shafts centred on a top-layer junction, breaking the surface and dropping 130-250 studs through several layers. a spiral ledge winds down the inside wall (6 studs wide, 28 studs per turn, about a 20 degree walk) with a torch every quarter turn. Cave.findShaft() takes you to one. (first go bent the shaft the same way the tunnels bend, so it wandered sideways and the view down was blocked after 68 studs. shafts are cut dead straight now)
- lava tubes: long round tunnels between two junctions 6-10 chunks apart in the deep layers, swaying and dipping gently. round cross-section and the wall bumps mostly left off so they look melted not dug, cracked glowing lava floor, basalt walls, an orange light every 18 studs. Cave.findTube() takes you to one
- geodes: hollow balls 14-20 studs across sealed in solid rock, the one thing that's meant not to connect. only placed where the ball and a 4 stud shell round it check out as rock at 27 points. violet crystal lining, about 70 glowing shards pointing in, one colour per geode, a light in the middle. a faint sparkle on the nearest tunnel wall is the clue. hollowed out after the pocket cleanup or it would fill them in. Cave.findGeode() takes you inside one
- smarter layout: it was still a grid underneath, every node linked east. now each 4x4 region of chunks gets a random spanning tree (kruskal on hashed weights), a few skipped links get dug anyway as loops, regions join their neighbours at least once and ramp down at least once. hubs (4+ tunnels) always get a chamber, caverns only go on busy junctions, dead ends half the time end in a small pocket. first go had too many extra links, 30% hubs and 6% dead ends, basically the grid again. turned it down to about 14% each. Cave.stats() gives dead ends, hubs, loops, caverns, km of tunnel and whether it's one piece: 5 seeds, 2304 junctions each, all one piece
- water that settles: the random puddle discs are gone. now each chunk looks for dips in the floor and spreads water sideways from them at that height. if it hits a drop, the chunk edge, a river or gets past 40 cells it would run away, so it stays dry, and only a dip walled in all round fills. pools in plain rock get a pale flowstone rim and floor. mineral streaks run down the walls (noise stretched tall and thin). seed 42 round the spawn: 40 pools, 0 water voxels hanging over air
- surface: the mine used to just stop at y 0 with nothing above it. now there's rolling hills over it (6 to about 50 studs up, two layers of noise), grass on top, earth under that, bare rock where it's steep. cave mouths: a straight sloping tunnel from a top level junction to the lowest ground within 110 studs, so it comes out of a hillside, about 25 degrees, with a timber frame and a lantern just inside. sinkholes now open at ground level. first go was night with moonlight, way too dark up there, so it's afternoon now. the sun and outdoor light only reach places that can see the sky so the caves stay dark, and underground the Atmosphere script drops the ambient back down and swaps in the cave fog. no ore or cave decor grows outside, pools up there don't get flowstone, and you can dig anywhere under the ground now. Cave.findEntrance() takes you to one. seed 42: mouth tunnel open at 30 of 31 points, ground where the code says at 192 of 200 columns
- the surface stopped 2 chunks out in a cliff over nothing, since chunks only load 2 around you. the ground layer now loads 10 chunks out (surfaceRadius) while you're near the top and stays loaded while you are. hills were nearly flat, the noise was too stretched, they go 6 to 34 studs now. pools don't form outside any more, they were cyan stickers on the grass. there was also a chunk-wide strip with no ground in it, couldn't get it back after the rebuild: 0 empty ground chunks round the mouth, and 77 of 6561 ground samples are holes, all sinkholes and cave mouths
- then a huge square pit in the grass straight down into a crystal cavern. caverns and chambers on the top level reach up to y 10-16, and the ground in the low bits is only 6 studs up, so they broke right through. first roof was 5 studs under the grass, too thin: the wall bumps and 4 stud voxels still poked holes, and far out, where only the ground layer is built, you looked down into caves with sky under them. now nothing cave-shaped goes above y -6 apart from sinkholes and mouths, so the ground layer is always solid, and the far load also builds 4 chunks down under every sinkhole and mouth so you never see sky at the bottom of one
- live editor: a studio plugin (plugin/CaveEditor.lua) with a seed box and sliders for tunnel and chamber size, caverns, loops, roughness, rivers, sinkholes, lava tubes, geodes, hill height and build radius. every change rebuilds round the camera from a fresh copy of the module, and a newer change stops a rebuild that's still going. 100 chunks take about 3 seconds. Cave.preview got split into Cave.prepare() and Cave.chunksAround() so the editor and timelapse build the same way
- debug views (CaveDebug): the network as balls and lines drawn through the rock (highlights, so they show through walls) coloured by junction type, a cut-away that clears everything on the camera's side of a flat cut, and a map of one level from above for the editor panel. seed 7: 162 junctions and 228 links drawn, cut-away cleared 15 of 15 samples in front and left 14 of 14 behind
- caves not tunnels (people on reddit said it was mostly tunnels, fair). a slow noise field widens passages by up to 20 studs so stretches open into galleries, about half the passages get an irregular room partway along, chambers are bigger and more common, caverns go on 1 in 5 busy junctions, and rooms are lumpy instead of clean balls. it only ever adds air round the same network so it's still one piece. seed 42, same block: air went from 16% to 33% of the rock, and the share of air more than 8 studs from a wall from 22% to 37%
- terrain looked like an old game (also reddit). the wall bumps had a 3 stud layer and thin 7 stud shelves, finer than the 4 stud terrain grid, so they came out as jagged random facets. bumps are big slow lumps now, shelves are gone, and the fine detail comes from rock outcrops instead: flat slabs set into walls, floors and ceilings, smooth shaded and texture mapped so they wear the same rock texture as the terrain. about 11 per chunk
- big spaces light themselves. the galleries came out black on video: lanterns only hang at junctions and a gallery swallows your lamp. glowworms now go on the ceiling of any space with 11+ studs of air under it, not just caverns, 1 patch in 7 carries a real light (was 1 in 17) with a longer reach, and walls of big spaces get clusters of glowing shelf fungus (amber, green or violet) with their own light. same plain rock block on seed 42: lit patches went from 106 to 260
- skylights: narrow cracks from the roof of a big space up through the grass, so daylight falls into the caves near the top. at most one per 160 stud square and only over a space with room under it (checked from the cave shape with the skylights left out, or finding one would need itself). the crack wanders a stud or so as it goes up. the real sun comes down it, and there's a faint shaft of light with dust in it plus a warm light where it lands so it shows even when the sun's at an angle. Cave.findSkylight() takes you under one. seed 42: crack open all 18 heights from the roof at -20 to the grass at 11, 7 built round the preview
- crystal cave floor: it was pale lilac and anything lit it white, it wrecked every crystal shot in the videos and looked the same in game. deep violet now, so the glowing crystals are what stands out
- pressing play did nothing. the spawn room built itself with preview(), and since the surface that also builds the ground 10 chunks out plus 4 chunks down under every sinkhole and mouth: 1337 chunks with no pause before anyone could spawn, and roblox stopped the script. it builds the 100 cave chunks round it now, pausing as it goes, 7.6s in studio. the ground loads round you after you spawn like everything else
- new spawn: outside on the hills a short walk from the nearest cave mouth, facing it. plank platform on legs so you're not on a slope, the controls on a signpost, crates, a lantern post, rails down into the mouth (first go floated them straight over the hole, they used the grass height and the mouth is carved out of it; now each sleeper finds the real floor and the rails carry on 40 studs down the tunnel), lanterns down the mouth tunnel and a bit of coal and iron just inside. the old underground room is still there as Cave.buildSpawnRoom() and gets used if there's no mouth nearby
- passage shapes: every passage used to be the same arch, just wider or narrower. now each one is one of four, the way real caves form: arched (flat floor, arched roof), round tubes (formed full of water), canyons (tall narrow slots a stream cut down) and keyholes (a round tube with a slot cut down under it). slots are never under 6 studs wide so you can walk them and the 4 stud grid doesn't pinch them shut. weights in settings.passageKinds. seed 42 over 1499 passages: 688 arched, 300 tube, 217 canyon, 294 keyhole. built a 3x3x3 chunk block and flood filled the voxels: nothing sealed off
- players with a CaveIgnore attribute don't load chunks round them or keep chunks loaded, so a game can park people in a lobby up in the sky without the mine building a ring of hills under it
- timelapse (CaveTimelapse): builds nearest chunks first with a pause between each, the camera swinging in front of a cut so you watch tunnels appear in cross-section, or a full orbit with the cut off
- pickaxe was held backwards, head behind you. flipped the grip, and the swing is now a slow lift and a fast strike instead of the other way round
- ores sit where the wall really is (blending the air/rock values) instead of the voxel edge, which was 1-3 studs behind the visible wall
